About Celevora

We're on a mission to make planning unforgettable events simple, transparent, and stress-free.

Our Story

Celevora started with a simple frustration: planning an event in Bangladesh meant juggling dozens of phone calls, unclear pricing, and vendors who were hard to compare side by side. We built Celevora to put everything — vendors, venues, packages, and payments — in one place, so anyone can plan a wedding, birthday, or corporate event with confidence.

Today, Celevora connects thousands of customers with verified vendors across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and beyond, helping turn ideas into events that people remember.
